Beginning with presence

A long drive can be treated as dead time. Companionship turns it into part of the pilgrimage. The expedition begins not at the trailhead but in the attention you give Vonda and the unhurried way you receive the day.

Question worth carrying: Can I enter this trip without mentally rushing ahead of the person beside me?

Bedtime: Psalm 4:8 - “In peace I will lie down and sleep.”

Receiving beauty without possessing it

A dune retains no footprint for long; the wind eventually erases every mark. That impermanence need not diminish the day. It can free you to receive beauty as gift rather than achievement.

Question worth carrying: Where does wonder become worship rather than consumption?

Fellowship under load

The strongest climber can make the day about pace. The formed climber makes it about presence: carrying a little more when needed, waiting without irritation, and refusing to make another person’s pace a moral verdict.

Question worth carrying: How can I make the approach better for someone else?

Achievement held with humility
