Immediate priorities

Lightning

Leave exposed summits, ridges, and isolated high points. Do not rely on the app for real-time lightning clearance.

Lost route

Stop, preserve battery, compare offline map/GPX, retrace only when the route is understood, and communicate early.

Injury or illness

Protect the person from further harm and exposure, assess communication and evacuation options, and escalate promptly when self-evacuation is unsafe.

Altitude symptoms

Worsening headache, vomiting, confusion, ataxia, severe breathlessness, or reduced responsiveness require descent and urgent escalation.

SOS

Use the inReach or emergency services when the situation warrants it. Provide location, problem, number of people, hazards, and resources available.

This is a compact field reference, not medical, rescue, or navigation certification.
