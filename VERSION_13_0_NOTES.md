# Version 13.0 — Climbing Journal and Expedition Archive

## Added

A searchable archive for completed or ended expedition sessions.

Each archived record can preserve:

- peak, route, date, partners, and summit result
- actual start, summit/high-point, turnaround, and finish times
- weather encountered
- gear used, unused, missing, or damaged
- food and water reality
- route and hazard observations
- what went well
- what should change next time
- favorite memory
- Scripture, prayer, or spiritual reflection
- photo references
- additional lessons
- Command Center field journal
- Decision Center responses
- communication-check snapshot
- nutrition-event log
- elapsed session time

## Search

Archive search covers mountain, route, partners, result, journal, weather, gear, lessons, memories, and spiritual reflections.

## Data

Archived records are stored under `ddmg-v13-expedition-archive`, remain device-local, and are included in unified app-data export through the `ddmg-` namespace.

## Preserved

All Version 12, 11, 10, 9.1, 9.0, and 8.6.1 functionality remains present.
