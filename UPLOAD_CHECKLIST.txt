DON DOWNS MOUNTAIN GUIDE — VERSION 5.1 AUDITED UPLOAD CHECKLIST

1. Download Don_Downs_Mountain_Guide_V5_1_Audited.zip.
2. Double-click the ZIP to extract it.
3. In GitHub, open DonDowns / mountain-guide.
4. Click Add file.
5. Click Upload files.
6. Drag ALL extracted Version 5 files onto the GitHub upload page.
7. Do not delete CNAME.
8. Commit message: Deploy audited Mountain Guide v5.1
9. Click Commit changes.
10. Wait 1–3 minutes.
11. Open https://mountainguide.vondadowns.com
12. Mac hard refresh if needed: Command + Shift + R.

IPHONE INSTALLATION

1. Open https://mountainguide.vondadowns.com in Safari.
2. Tap the Share icon.
3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
4. Keep the name Mountain Guide.
5. Tap Add.
6. Open the new Mountain Guide icon once while online.
7. Tap the weather refresh button and Refresh Trip Intelligence.
8. Close the app, turn on Airplane Mode, and reopen it to verify offline access.
