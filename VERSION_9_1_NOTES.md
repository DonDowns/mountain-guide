# Version 9.1 — Generated Itinerary and Readiness

## Added

For the active Trip Builder record, Version 9.1 generates:

- a schedule and timing blueprint
- route-review context
- food and water planning estimates
- communication preparation
- forecast-horizon status
- trip-specific readiness checks

## Readiness discipline

Generated checks are stored inside the trip record on this device. Completing them confirms only that the item was checked; it does not certify that the route, equipment, weather, or group is safe.

## Schedule estimates

The generated summit midpoint and route-finish times use a conservative planning formula based on published mileage, elevation gain, route class, and commitment. They are explicitly labeled as estimates.

## Food and water

The app provides planning starting points rather than individualized prescriptions. Actual needs depend on conditions, refill reliability, exertion, and the user.

## Preserved

All Version 9.0 and 8.6.1 functionality remains present, including the protected Lake Como field workflow.
