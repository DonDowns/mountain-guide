# Version 7.8 — Gear Advisor

## Added

Version 7.8 adds a plan-based Gear Advisor in the gear/readiness area.

The advisor reads:

- the active pack preset
- whether the plan is an overnight, day hike, Lake Como trip, or summit-repack plan
- saved weather/forecast flags when available
- active pack confirmations
- gear status and source actions

It gives conservative recommendations for:

- heavy approach pack vs summit pack
- single vs double 14er margin
- long/exposed objectives
- cold/wind/precipitation forecast margin
- possible snow/ice/traction decisions
- shared/borrowed/verify gear items

## Safety wording

The advisor is a planning aid only. It does not certify that gear, weather, route, group condition, or timing is safe.
