# Version 6.6 — Personal Gear Locker and Smart Pack Builder

## Purpose

Version 6.6 converts the Lake Como packing checklist into a reusable personal equipment system.

## Personal Gear Locker

The locker is seeded with known gear and preferences, including:

- Gregory Paragon 60
- REI Flash 18
- Osprey Manta
- Sierra Designs Sweet Suite 2
- Tepui two-person rooftop tent
- Audi Q5 as the default travel vehicle
- Garmin Forerunner 965
- Garmin inReach
- hydration reservoirs and bottles
- water filter
- rented 20°F sleeping bag and insulated pad
- borrowed or rented trekking poles
- preferred trail food and dehydrated meals

Each item supports persistent availability, default location, optional weight, and a note.

## Smart Pack Builder presets

- Lake Como 2026
- Colorado 14er Summit Day
- Overnight Backpack + Summit
- Audi Q5 + Tepui Rooftop Camp
- Mountain Travel + Lodging

Lake Como contains distinct stages for worn items, the Gregory approach pack, the REI Flash summit repack, Audi/lodging support, shared assignments, and conditional gear.

## Future use

Custom gear can be added to the locker and active preset. Gear records and pack checks are included in the app backup, which can be imported onto another device.

## Safety and scope

The Audi Q5 is the default travel vehicle, but the app does not assume it is appropriate for the roughest Lake Como Road segment. The exact Tepui model and roof-system ratings are not stored; the app requires verification before rooftop-tent use.
