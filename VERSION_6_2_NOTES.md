# Version 6.2 — Daily Formation Edition

## Purpose

Version 6.2 adds a deliberate closing rhythm to every expedition day without adding content to the main dashboard or Summit Focus.

Each itinerary day now contains:

- Morning or summit Scripture
- A short formation thought
- A morning question
- Evening Scripture
- A short reflection appropriate to finishing the day
- An evening question designed to help release the day and rest

The morning and evening cards appear only after a day is opened, so the main app remains uncluttered.

## Additional correction

The Summit Goal card now reads **38 / 50 projected after the planned trip**, with a mathematically correct **76%** progress bar.
